## About This Site

Picture this: A 30-something Brazilian engineer who decided to turn his brain's overflow into a digital sanctuary. That's me, and this is my attempt at both preserving knowledge and sharing it with fellow curious minds.

## Why This Website Exists

I launched this digital space in January 2025 with two missions in mind: First, to give back to the incredible online community that's been freely sharing knowledge for years. Second (and let's be honest here), because I've crossed the big 3-0, and my brain isn't what it used to be.

## What You'll Find Here

- **Technical documentation**: Guides and tutorials on self-hosting, Docker, Raspberry Pi, and more
- **Personal reflections**: My journey through graduate school, career challenges, and lessons learned
- **Projects**: Interesting things I'm building

## About Me

I'm an engineer passionate about technology, privacy, and automation. When I'm not coding, you'll find me reading about AI and data science, playing with my Raspberry Pi, or trying to balance privacy and productivity.
