Welcome to My Digital Garden 🌱

Picture this: A 30-something Brazilian engineer who decided to turn his brain’s overflow into a digital sanctuary. That’s me, and this is my attempt at both preserving knowledge and sharing it with fellow curious minds.### Why This Website ExistsI launched this digital space in January 2025 with two missions in mind: First, to give back to the incredible online community that’s been freely sharing knowledge for years. Second (and let’s be honest here), because I’ve crossed the big 3-0, and my brain isn’t the reliable storage device it used to be. Future Théo will thank me for this digital memory bank.Here, you’ll primarily find tech-related content, but I also sprinkle in various life lessons and discoveries that I’ve found too valuable to forget. Don’t expect rigid posting schedules or overly polished content. Instead, I’ll share my complete thought processes and the stories behind my discoveries – because understanding how someone reached a solution can be just as valuable as the solution itself.### The Face Behind the ScreenI’m Théo Bredemann, a Brazilian electronic engineer who’s ventured into the realms of Python programming and artificial intelligence. While my day job revolves around telemetry systems (particularly in the energy sector), my career path has taken some interesting turns – including a stint as a cameraman at the 2014 World Cup. Yes, really!I’ve always gravitated toward teaching and sharing knowledge. Consider me your informal tech guide, offering free insights and instructions without the classroom formality. Fell free to stalk me on LinkedIn.### The Technical BitsThis site runs on Ghost, an open-source platform, and lives on my trusty Raspberry Pi. Why? Because it aligns perfectly with my mission to spread knowledge freely and openly. You won’t find any cookies, trackers, or unnecessary bells and whistles here – just pure, unfiltered content for those patient enough to explore.Fair warning: Posts may meander through detailed explanations and behind-the-scenes stories. If you’re looking for quick fixes, you might need to exercise some patience. But I promise the journey will be worth it.—This is a living document, updated whenever interesting life events demand it.
